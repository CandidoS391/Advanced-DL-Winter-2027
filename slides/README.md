# Slides

This directory is the space in which all meeting slides will be posted throughout the project. They will not only guide how the meeting will proceed, but will also be the main source of content/information regarding the project, with the notebooks/other resources found within the general repository helping to supplement them.

Each week's content will be posted here before each meeting so that participants have a preview of the week in terms of the project. Likewise those who need to miss the week's meeting will always have the opportunity to review the material and catch-up without needing to ask.
