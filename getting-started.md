## 2. Setting Up Your Development Environment

### Kaggle (Recommended)
For this project, the project leads will be using a platform called Kaggle, which is a completely free and browser-based AI/data science platform similar to Google Colab. More importantly, Kaggle offers more computing power for what we are doing without needing to pay compared to Colab, with Kaggle offering access to two Tesla T4 GPUs for 30 hours per week. With that being said, here's how to set up your own Kaggle account.

<ins>**Steps to setting up Kaggle**</ins>
1. Visit the Kaggle website [here](https://www.kaggle.com/).
2. Sign up using your email/Google account and go through any email verification steps. It should also be stated that you should preferably use a personal account when signing up so that your work won't be deleted when you leave UCR.
3. Once logged on, go to your settings page and verify your phone number. This is essential to getting access to those complimentary GPUs. In terms of identity verification, you **do not** have to take part in that in terms of this project.
